# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

This policy covers the Flow Steward WhatsApp extension (`flowsteward.whatsapp`) and the
Meta app that connects it to the WhatsApp Business Platform.

## What the extension is

The extension is open-source software that each user installs and runs on their own
Flow Steward server. The authors do not operate a hosted service for it and do not
receive any data that passes through it.

## Data processed

When you connect your own WhatsApp Business account, the extension on your server
handles:

- messages sent to your WhatsApp business number (sender phone number, message text,
  media identifiers, timestamps);
- messages and delivery statuses your workflows send from that number;
- the access token and app secret you enter, stored encrypted in your own Flow Steward
  installation.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to run the workflows you configure: starting a workflow when a
message arrives and sending the replies you define. It is not sold, shared with third
parties or used for advertising.

## Storage and retention

All data stays on your own server and in your own Flow Steward database. You control
how long it is kept and can delete it at any time by removing the connection, the
contacts or the workflow history. Messages are also processed by WhatsApp / Meta under
Meta's own terms and privacy policy.

## Third parties

The extension only talks to the WhatsApp Cloud API operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.
No analytics or tracking services are used.

## Your rights and contact

Because the data lives on your server, you are its controller and can access or delete
it directly. For questions about this policy contact hello@piksis.eu.
